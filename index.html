In preparationManuscript

Unsupervised differentiation of glioma recurrence and radiation necrosis via continuous flow matching of anatomical trajectories

M. M. Uddin, A. Jumma, M. Y. S. Emu

After radiotherapy, a growing lesion on an MRI can be the tumour coming back or tissue damaged by the treatment, and on a single scan the two look nearly the same. TDFlow, our model, looks at how the anatomy moves between scans instead. A 3D U-Net flow matching model, trained on 3,000 healthy and 2,637 longitudinal patient scans, produces deformation features that a ConvNeXt-3D and Mamba encoder clusters without any labels.

CitizenLink: scalable graph-based modeling and analysis of multi-generational citizen relationships

A. Jumma, A. Bormon, A. A. Zishan, R. Palit

Bangladesh's civil registry stores people as rows, yet many of the questions asked of it are about relationships. Who are this person's legal heirs? Was this child registered twice? CitizenLink models parent, child and marital ties as a graph, which supports family trees, inheritor identification, lineage tracing and duplicate birth registration detection, all queries that record-oriented databases handle poorly.
