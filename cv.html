CitizenLink: Scalable Graph-Based Modeling and Analysis of Multi-Generational Citizen Relationships

A. Jumma, A. Bormon, A. A. Zishan, and R. Palit

Models Bangladesh's civil registry as a graph of parent, child and marital ties, enabling multi-generational queries that record-oriented databases handle poorly. Supports family trees, inheritor identification, lineage tracing and duplicate birth registration detection, with 13.0 ms NID lookups and sub-100 ms relationship queries across 5.13M citizens and 10.16M relationships.

Contribution: Refined the core idea, optimized query speed, and wrote the paper and its figures.

In preparation

Unsupervised Differentiation of Glioma Recurrence and Radiation Necrosis via Continuous Flow Matching of Anatomical Trajectories

M. M. Uddin, A. Jumma, and M. Y. S. Emu

TDFlow separates glioma recurrence from radiation necrosis by modeling how anatomy changes over time, not how a single MRI looks. A 3D U-Net flow matching model, trained on 3,000 healthy and 2,637 longitudinal patient scans, produces deformation features that a ConvNeXt-3D and Mamba encoder clusters without labels. Without biopsy labels it reached AUC 0.864 versus 0.812 for a supervised 3D ResNet-18, and held up on an external cohort.

Contribution: Proposed the idea, prepared the data, building the model, and writing the manuscript.
